CV

I am a Research Assistant Professor (postdoc) at the Simons Center for Geometry and Physics. Before this I was a postdoctoral member at the Institute for Advanced Study during 2024-26 and a visiting fellow at the Max Planck Institute for Mathematics, Bonn during the year 2023-24. I received my PhD from Columbia University in May 2023 under the supervision of Mohammed Abouzaid. My research focuses on Symplectic topology and Floer theory. Specifically, I am interested in the study of algebraic structures underlying various Floer theoretic invariants. Currently, I'm thinking about relations between algebraic invariants defined using Floer theory and Symplectic Field theory.

I am co-organizing the SCGP/SBU Symplectic Geometry, Gauge Theory, and Low-Dimensional Topology Seminar. In 2023-24 I co-organized the Bonn symplectic seminar. In Fall 2022 I co-organized the Microlocal sheaf theory seminar at Columbia.
