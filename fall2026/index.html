This course prepares students for the regular calculus sequences through the study of functions and modeling. Topics include rational, exponential, logarithmic, and trigonometric functions; asymptotic behavior and limits; and an introduction to slope, derivatives, linear approximation, and curve sketching.

Prerequisite: C or better in MAP 103, or level 3 on the mathematics placement examination, completed within one year before the course begins.
